CivicFix Architecture & Impact Presentation

Eliminating Redundant Municipal Dispatches Across 100+ Smart Cities

Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) receive thousands of duplicate complaints daily after monsoons or civic disruptions. CivicFix intercepts duplicates at the source, clustering reports within a 35-meter radius into a single actionable Master Ticket.

Spatial Geo-Clustering

Uses the spherical Haversine formula to detect reports within 35 meters of an existing open hazard.

Semantic NLP Guard

Prevents false merges by verifying category alignment and headline keyword overlap before clustering.

Unified Audit Trail

Preserves every citizen's uploaded photo inside the Master Ticket for tamper-proof SLA verification.

Legacy Municipal Workflow

  • • 15 commuters report the same pothole → 15 separate ticket IDs generated.
  • • 3 different junior engineers inspect the same spot on consecutive days.
  • • High fuel wastage, inflated backlog metrics, and delayed closure.

CivicFix v3.10 Workflow

  • • Report #1 creates Master Ticket #CF-1082.
  • • Reports #2–#15 are intercepted in real time (≤35m) and merged as upvotes + photo proofs.
  • • 1 PWD crew repairs the pothole and resolves all 15 citizen trackers in one click.

Mathematical Deduplication Pipeline

1. Great-Circle Haversine Formula:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)·cos(φ2)·sin²(Δλ/2)
d = 2 · R · atan2(√a, √(1−a))  // Trigger if d ≤ 35m

2. NLP Token Overlap & Category Match:

Tokenizes incoming headlines, strips stop-words, and computes Jaccard/Cosine keyword overlap against open tickets in the same civic category or within 35 meters.
